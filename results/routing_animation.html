Replay cost as training proceeds

Cost vs optimum is the mean, over the 60 replay steps, of social cost divided by the optimum at that step's background load. The equilibrium gap is the most any one agent could gain by switching to its best response against the other three policies (0 means an equilibrium).

Every number here comes from the project's Python code: network.simulate_network for costs, game_theory.py for the optimum and Nash equilibria. Means over five seeds are in results/tables/main_comparison.csv.
